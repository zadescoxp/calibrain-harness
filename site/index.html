Does your model know the market?Ask it. Keep score.

Calibrain Harness puts a fast classification model on live crypto perpetuals, asks it what happens next on every bar, trades the confident answers on a Bybit demo account, and checks every forecast against what the market actually did.

A walk through the terminal

Live markets and the model's signal, then the screens that tell you whether any of it works: evaluation, validation, trades, news and the log.

A loop that runs on every closed bar

Questions the market answers within hours, so every forecast can be checked, traded or not.

1

Ask

The model sees candles, order flow and funding, plus how often each outcome really happens, and answers questions like "+1.0% or −0.6% first?"

2

Decide

Probabilities become expected value after fees and slippage. Only trades that clear the edge, quality and risk gates go through.

3

Trade

Long or short on Bybit Demo Trading against the real market, with a stop and a target from the first second.

4

Score

When the horizon passes, each forecast is labelled and scored against the base rate. A model that adds nothing stops driving trades.

Built to learn, not to risk your savings

Demo accounts only

Orders only go to Bybit Demo Trading or the Bybit Testnet. Both are hard-coded; there is no switch to live funds.

Keys stay on your computer

Saved in a private folder, never logged or stored in the database. The app only shows their last four characters.

Starts by watching

Forecasts are made and scored without orders until you turn on auto-trade. Stop all and a daily loss limit halt everything.

Install it and start watching your model work

No Python, no terminal, no config files. Open the app, paste your model and Bybit Demo keys, and the first forecasts arrive at the next bar close.
