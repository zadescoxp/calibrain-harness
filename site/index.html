Does your model know the market?Ask it. Then keep score.

Calibrain Harness puts a fast classification model on live crypto perpetuals. Every few minutes it asks the model what happens next, trades the confident answers on a Bybit demo account, and checks every forecast against what the market actually did.

One minute inside the terminal

Live candles and order book, the model's signal with its expected value after costs, and the evaluation screen that tells you whether any of it is working.

A loop that runs on every closed bar

Instead of asking a model for a buy or sell call, the harness asks questions that the market will answer within hours. That makes every forecast checkable, traded or not.

  1. 1

    Ask

    The model sees the candles, order flow and funding, plus how often each outcome really happens. Then it answers questions like "which comes first in the next two hours: +1.0% or −0.6%?"

  2. 2

    Decide

    Its probabilities become an expected value after fees and slippage. Only trades that clear the edge, quality and risk gates go through. The rest are still recorded.

  3. 3

    Trade

    Long or short on Bybit Demo Trading, against the real market, with a protected stop and target from the first second. Position size follows your risk per trade.

  4. 4

    Score

    When the horizon passes, each forecast is labelled with what happened and scored. The calibration layer learns from it, so a model that adds nothing stops driving trades.

Honest numbers, not a backtest

Every answer is checked against the market, so you see what the model really knows, not what a backtest with hindsight suggests.

Measured forward, in real time

Forecasts are made before the outcome exists and labelled afterwards from mainnet prices.

Skill against the base rate

Brier and RPS skill compare the model with simply knowing how often each outcome happens.

Real execution costs

Fees, slippage and funding are booked at the real-market price, even when the demo venue is kinder.

Bring your own model

Kairos and Jev work out of the box. Each model is scored separately, so results never mix.

Built to learn, not to risk your savings

  1. Demo accounts only

    Orders only ever go to Bybit Demo Trading or the Bybit Testnet. Both are hard-coded, and there is no switch that points the bot at live funds.

  2. Your keys stay on your computer

    Keys are saved in a private folder on your machine. They are never logged, never stored in the database, and the app only ever shows their last four characters.

  3. Starts in watch mode

    The bot makes and scores forecasts without placing orders until you switch on auto-trade. A kill switch and a daily loss limit stop everything at once.

Install it and start watching your model work

No Python, no terminal commands, no config files. Open the app, paste your model and Bybit Demo keys, and the first forecasts arrive at the next bar close.
